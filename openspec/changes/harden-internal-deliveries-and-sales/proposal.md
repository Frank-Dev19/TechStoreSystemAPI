# Harden internal material delivery and service-order sales

Material exits must follow the physical service workflow. Quote-backed products can leave inventory only while the equipment is in service, and the equipment cannot be resolved until the current accepted quote has a complete, confirmed reconciliation. Manual technician supplies may optionally reference a service order while remaining outside its quote.

The same change aligns operational UI behavior: cash payments use the payable amount rounded to S/ 0.10 while non-cash payments remain exact; product selectors search the server after three characters with a 500 ms debounce; Kardex labels show Spanish business concepts; receptionists can read the supervision panel; and order panels expose a manual refresh action.
