# Requirements
- Intake MUST identify the person regardless of service type; Assembly MUST NOT silently become an internal order without customer.
- Labor waiver MUST retain original labor value and record Servicio interno, actor and timestamp with net labor zero.
- Waiver MUST reject product lines, unknown reasons, missing authorization, and simultaneous ordinary discounts.
- Quote totals and service sales MUST use net amounts; material stock processing MUST remain unchanged.
- Existing drafts MUST NOT reactivate clientless internal intake.
